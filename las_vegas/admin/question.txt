Main Intelligence Question:

How effectively can Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery distinguish urban development, desert/bare land, vegetation, 
and major infrastructure across the Las Vegas urban–desert interface?


Primary Imagery:

Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface-reflectance imagery.

Primary Acquisition Period:

Summer 2026.

The exact acquisition date will be selected based on imagery coverage, cloud conditions, and overall image quality.


Supporting Questions:

1. What major land-cover types can be identified in natural-color Sentinel-2 imagery?

2. Which features become easier to distinguish using NIR- and SWIR-based false-color composites?

3. Where is healthy or irrigated vegetation concentrated within the predominantly urban and desert environment?

4. How clearly can built-up surfaces be distinguished from surrounding desert and bare land?

5. How do vegetation, built-up surfaces, and desert surfaces differ in their visible, NIR, and SWIR spectral responses?

6. What ambiguities or limitations remain when interpreting the area using 10–20 m Sentinel-2 imagery?


Analytical Focus:

The project will compare natural-color imagery, false-color composites, spectral indices, visual image interpretation, and representative spectral responses.

The analysis focuses on one primary imagery period rather than temporal change.


Excluded From Scope:

- Multi-year change detection
- Urban expansion analysis
- Advanced image classification
- Advanced terrain analysis
- Automated monitoring
- Large geospatial programming workflows
