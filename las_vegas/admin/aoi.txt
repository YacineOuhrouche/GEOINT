AREA OF INTEREST

Study Area:
Las Vegas Valley, Nevada, USA

Administrative Context:
Clark County, Nevada, United States

Study Topic:
Urban–desert interface of the Las Vegas metropolitan area.

AOI Description:
The study area covers the central Las Vegas metropolitan area and surrounding urban–desert interface. It includes dense urban development, residential areas, major transportation infrastructure, Harry Reid International Airport, irrigated vegetation, and surrounding arid/bare land.

Reason for Selection:
Las Vegas provides a clear contrast between built-up urban surfaces and surrounding desert land. The area also contains irrigated vegetation, transportation infrastructure, residential development, and other visually distinct land-cover types, making it well suited for Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery analysis.
