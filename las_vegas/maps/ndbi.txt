NDBI OBSERVATIONS

Index:
NDBI = (B11 - B08) / (B11 + B08)

Observed Range:
Approximately -0.65 to 0.54

1. Positive NDBI values occur across extensive portions of the surrounding desert.

2. The Las Vegas urban area contains a mixture of positive, near-zero, and negative NDBI values.

3. The urbanized area is not uniformly separable from surrounding bare desert using NDBI alone.

4. Vegetated locations generally show lower NDBI values than dry surrounding surfaces.

5. Water and some low-reflectance surfaces show strongly negative NDBI values.

6. Desert terrain and built-up surfaces can produce similar NDBI responses.

7. NDBI therefore provides useful spectral information but cannot independently classify urban development in this arid environment.

8. Combining NDBI with natural color, false-color imagery, NDVI, shape, texture, and spatial context is necessary for more confident interpretation.
