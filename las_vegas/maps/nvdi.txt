NDVI OBSERVATIONS

Index:
NDVI = (B08 - B04) / (B08 + B04)

Observed Range:
Approximately -0.50 to 0.69

1. Most of the Las Vegas metropolitan area has relatively low NDVI values.

2. Distinct high-NDVI patches occur throughout the urban area.

3. These higher-NDVI areas are concentrated in regularly shaped landscaped areas, parks, golf courses, and other irrigated locations.

4. The surrounding desert generally has low NDVI values.

5. A clear linear high-NDVI feature is visible east of the metropolitan area.

6. Some mountainous areas show moderate vegetation response compared with the surrounding desert.

7. NDVI distinguishes vegetated surfaces much more clearly than the natural-color image.

8. The strongest vegetation signals are localized rather than continuous across the metropolitan region.
