PROJECT:
Salt Lake County Regional Geographic Intelligence Analysis


MAIN INTELLIGENCE QUESTION

Why did Salt Lake City and the surrounding Salt Lake County region develop
where they did, and how do terrain, hydrology, population, transportation,
and infrastructure explain the region's present spatial structure?


SUPPORTING QUESTIONS

1. What terrain features most strongly constrain development in Salt Lake County?

2. How do elevation and slope vary between the valley floor and surrounding mountains?

3. How have the Great Salt Lake, Jordan River, and mountain drainage systems influenced settlement and development?

4. Where is population most concentrated, and how does this relate to terrain and elevation?

5. Which major transportation corridors dominate regional connectivity?

6. How do highways and rail corridors relate to the valley floor, mountain barriers, and canyon corridors?

7. Where are the major geographic barrier crossings and constrained transportation corridors?

8. Where are industrial and logistics activities concentrated, and how do they relate to highways, rail, and Salt Lake City International Airport?

9. Which parts of the county are most accessible through the transportation network?

10. How have the Wasatch Range, Oquirrh Mountains, Great Salt Lake, and valley geography shaped the overall urban form of the region?


PROJECT SCOPE

The project will analyze the interaction between physical geography
and human systems using terrain, hydrology, population, land use,
transportation, infrastructure, accessibility, and network data.

The project will remain a static geographic-intelligence analysis.

Formal multi-date change detection, advanced remote sensing,
OSINT investigation, database development, and web-dashboard development
are outside the scope of this project.
